Privacy

How we look after the details you share with us.

What we collect

When you book a table or send us a message, we collect the details you give us: usually your name, phone number, email address, booking date, time, number of guests and any notes such as dietary needs.

How we use it

  • To confirm and manage your booking or reply to your message.
  • To prepare for any dietary needs or special requests you mention.

We don't sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

How it's stored

Booking requests and messages arrive in our business email inbox and are kept only as long as we need them.

Third-party services

Our website shows maps from Google Maps and uses Google Fonts. These services may collect technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Email gurusindiancuisine@gmail.com or call (02) 6613 0600.
