Our sister store

A one-stop shop for your kitchen

Om Namah is a 100% Australian-owned small business, started in December 2012 by Sonal and Som. When they first moved to Coffs Harbour they had to bring Indian groceries from Brisbane every month, so they opened a store to bring authentic food to the local community, and a taste of home to the Indian community.

We stock authentic Indian, Sri Lankan, Pakistani, Persian and African products at reasonable, competitive prices. Our team believes in sharing not just the products, but the knowledge behind them.
