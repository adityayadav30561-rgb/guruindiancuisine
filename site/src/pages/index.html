Our Story

A family kitchen with a 5-star chef

Guru's is run by Sonal Arora and chef Somnath "Som" Bhaskar, who were born and raised in India and found their home in Coffs Harbour. Som spent 14 years cooking in prestigious 5-star hotels like the Oberoi. Sonal runs the restaurant.

Their mission is simple: share the intriguing flavours of a centuries-old family tradition, and help you explore, indulge and even recreate them at home.
