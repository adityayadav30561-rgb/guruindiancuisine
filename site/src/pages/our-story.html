Our Story

Masters of Indian Cuisine

A family kitchen in Coffs Harbour, built on centuries-old recipes and a chef's 14 years in India's finest hotels.

Meet the family

Sonal & Som

Guru's Indian Restaurant is owned and run by Sonal Arora and Somnath "Som" Bhaskar, who were born and raised in India and found their new home in Coffs Harbour.

Som is our chef, with 14 years of professional experience in prestigious 5-star hotels such as the Oberoi. Sonal is the restaurateur who looks after every guest who walks through the door.

The inspiration for Guru's came from being captivated by the beauty of the region, and a heartfelt desire to share India's exotic cuisine, fresh from the kitchen.

Sonal & Som

How it began

From India to the Coffs Coast

  1. 2008

    Sonal and Som arrive in Australia.

  2. 2011

    Driving to Sydney, they pass through Coffs Harbour. It feels so welcoming, with such beautiful scenery, that they decide this will be home.

  3. 2012

    They move to Coffs Harbour, but have to bring their Indian groceries all the way from Brisbane every month.

  4. 3 December 2012

    They open Om Namah Indian Grocery so the whole community can cook authentic Indian food at home.

  5. Today

    Guru's serves Som's family recipes, freshly cooked, at 10 Park Avenue. Their mission hasn't changed: to help guests explore, indulge in and even recreate the flavours of India.
